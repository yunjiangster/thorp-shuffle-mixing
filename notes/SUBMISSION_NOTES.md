# arXiv submission notes

**Title:** A subquadratic upper bound for the mixing time of the Thorp shuffle

**Author:** Yunjiang Jiang

**Suggested primary category:** math.PR (Probability)

**Possible secondary category:** math.CO (Combinatorics)

**Main theorem:** For `n=2^d` and every fixed `0<epsilon<1`, the worst-start total-variation mixing time of the full labelled Thorp shuffle is

`t_mix(epsilon) <= C_* d^(5/3) + O_epsilon(d^(4/3) log(d+2))`,

with `C_*=5.153695145414931...`.

This expository revision is self-contained in `main.tex`. It contains no external figures, data files, or BibTeX dependencies. The bibliography is embedded. It was compiled repeatedly with `pdflatex`, preflighted, and rendered for visual inspection.

The exposition now includes a detailed physical-to-hypercube derivation, an eight-card example, the rotating-frame identity, a first-time-reader dictionary, and plain-language introductions to every major proof module and technical appendix. The proof itself is retained in full.

The paper states two external black-box inputs explicitly and cites their sources: strong-Rayleigh negative dependence (Borcea-Branden-Liggett) and the high-level symmetric-group dimension estimate used from Keevash-Lifshitz. All Thorp-specific analytic estimates are proved in the manuscript.
